Keep the look. Rewrite the pages.

The brand book and the Framer look are worth keeping. The code under the public site is not: 26 public URLs are Framer HTML pasted into route files (11.3 MB of strings). They cannot connect to the dashboard, the CMS, Arabic RTL or the booking flow. The React design system is the right base, but it grew by patches and needs one consolidation pass before any page is rebuilt on it.

Keep that design?
YesKEEP

Monogram, wordmark, teal / charcoal / gold / ivory, Questa + Lato, square corners, full-bleed photography, ivory editorial sections. It fits private luxury travel and it is Khadija's identity. Do not redesign it.

Yes, the systemCONSOLIDATE

Not the logo or palette. The web layer: one teal, one ivory, one gold, one type scale, gold never as text on ivory, a dense variant for the dashboard, Team only from the dashboard CMS, logos served from the repo instead of Framer's CDN.

Fully change it?
NoDON'T

The problem is the code, not the look. A new visual direction adds weeks of design and client sign-off, and still leaves the same rewrite. You already picked the Framer look (D-51).
