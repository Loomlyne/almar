Logical CSS only (inline-start / inline-end). Panels open from the same segment, mirrored. Search icon flips. Dates stay DD/MM/YYYY with Western numerals. Uppercase tracking is dropped for Arabic labels.

Rules

• Square corners everywhere. No pills. • Gold is the 2px bar rule only. Never text on ivory or white. • Search validates, it is never disabled. Missing fields get the error rule and one alert line. • Search never charges. The first charge is step 4. • Every value, label and alt in EN / AR / ES. No hard-coded copy in components. • Destinations, add-ons, inclusions and prices come from the dashboard. Nothing seeded in the component. • Touch targets 44 px minimum. Visible focus: 3px teal rule inside the segment, 2px teal outline elsewhere. • One source: the hero, phone sheet and /booking/trip summary are the same component in three sizes.
