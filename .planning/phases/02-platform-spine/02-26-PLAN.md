## Task 2 — forwarded-link check (after the owner signs the pictures)

`requestSignIn` sets an httpOnly, SameSite=Lax, 1-hour cookie with a random nonce; the link carries
`HMAC(continue key, nonce)` as `b`. On the Continue page: cookie matches `b` → the signed design as is.
No match (another browser, or a forwarded link) → the same page plus an email field; `confirmSignIn` compares
the typed email (normalised) with the link's full email, proven by the server (the link carries the email
HMAC, never the email). Wrong email → refused with a line under the field, token not spent.
