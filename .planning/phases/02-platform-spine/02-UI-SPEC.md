## Spacing Scale

Declared values (must be multiples of 4):

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4px | Icon gaps, inline padding |
| sm | 8px | Compact element spacing, gap inside a group |
| md | 16px | Default element spacing, gap between groups |
| lg | 24px | Section padding |
| xl | 32px | Layout gaps |
| 2xl | 48px | Major section breaks |
| 3xl | 64px | Page-level spacing |

Exceptions: none.

Hit targets, radii, borders, breakpoints, and type sizes are not spacing tokens. They are specified outside this section.

Group with space, not lines. The gap between groups is at least twice the gap inside a group: 8px inside, 16px between. A hairline is only for a control border, a selected state, or a divider space cannot carry. Use logical properties (`padding-inline`, `margin-inline-start`, `inset-inline-start`). Do not use physical left or right for layout.

## Typography

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 |
| Label | 14px | 400 | 1.4 |
| Heading | 32px | 400 | 1.1 |
| Display | 48px | 400 | 1.1 |

Exactly four sizes. Exactly two weights: 400 and 700.

- 400 is the weight of every role above. Questa is Regular only. Do not synthesize a Questa bold. Noto Naskh Arabic is loaded at 400 only. Do not synthesize an Arabic title bold.
- 700 is Lato and Noto Sans Arabic only. Use it for the grand-total pattern already in the system, not as a fifth size and not as a third weight. Do not load Lato Hairline, Thin, Light, Medium, Semibold, Heavy, or Black.
- Italic is a style of Lato 400, not a third weight.
- Display and heading tracking: `-0.02em`. Body tracking: `0`. Label tracking: `0`, except the live header, which keeps its current Framer tracking. See [Live header](#live-header).
- Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. Descriptions that wrap use `text-wrap: pretty`. Long emails and IDs use `overflow-wrap: break-word`.
- Store copy in natural case. Uppercase on the live bar and on the signed-in row is `text-transform: uppercase` only. Do not store `SIGN IN` or `TOUCHWORD` as the source string.
- Inputs are 16px on viewports below `48rem`, so iOS does not zoom. From `48rem` up, inputs are the label size. Do not use `transform: scale()` to fake the size.
- Prices, VAT, deposit, the send-again countdown, and the live FX figure use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Dates use Western numerals, `DD/MM/YYYY`. The week starts Monday.
- Arabic sets `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"` on the document. Spanish and English are `ltr`. Isolate a mixed-direction email or amount with `<bdi>`.

The live header’s current type is Lato 14px, weight 400, line-height 1.5. That is the Label role. Do not add a fifth size to match it.

## Color

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `#fffaf0` | Page, maintenance page, ops page, empty surfaces |
| Secondary (30%) | `#ffffff` | Cards, inputs, the open dropdown list, ops side column |
| Accent (10%) | `#d4ba8a` | The reserved-for list only. Never body text. |
| Destructive | `#8f2d2d` | Danger outline only. Not a fill. Label on that control stays charcoal. |

60% ivory / 30% white / 10% gold. One accent. Teal `#1f3b40` is heading and link ink, and the focus ring. It is not a second accent. Charcoal `#262626` is body text. It is also the covering-menu surface on the public header, because that menu must keep light type. Status green `#1e6b45` and warning `#8a3b12` are not accents and are not brand pickers.

Accent reserved for:

- The one primary action fill on a view
- The open `/design` dropdown’s selected inset
- The gold hairline under a page title on the maintenance page only

Not reserved for: the live header `Sign in` control, every link, every icon, the focus ring, body text, secondary buttons, the selected tab, nav items, danger buttons, or a logo clear control.

Primary action fill is gold. Label is teal. Measured this session (sRGB relative luminance): teal `#1f3b40` on gold `#d4ba8a` is 6.37:1. Hover fill is the existing `--color-accent-hover` `#c6ae82`, teal on it 5.56:1. Press fill is `--color-accent-press` `#b8a27a`, teal on it 4.83:1. Never ivory or white on gold (1.80:1 and 1.88:1). Never gold as text on ivory (1.80:1).

Danger control: transparent fill, charcoal label, 1px error outline. Charcoal on ivory is 14.55:1. Error outline on ivory is 7.82:1. Not a red fill. Not a gold fill.

## Field errors

The line sits under the field, not only under the label. `aria-invalid="true"`. `aria-describedby` points at the line. Focus the first invalid field on submit. Keep the gold button enabled until the request starts, then disable it, keep the label, and show the existing square spinner in `currentColor`. The settings Save control is the exception: it stays disabled until a change exists.

Do not use color alone. The text is the fix.

| Case | Line |
|------|------|
| Name empty | Add your name. |
| Last name empty | Add your last name. |
| Name or last name has anything but letters, Arabic letters, accents, spaces, or hyphens | Use letters, spaces, or hyphens. |
| Email empty or not an email | Enter an email address. |
| Phone filled with anything but digits and a plus | Use digits and a plus only. |

## Interaction

- Native `button` for actions. Native `a` for navigation. A real link supports modified clicks. touchword is an `a` with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Its accessible name is touchword. Do not add “dashboard” to that name.
- One primary action per view. Peers are text or a charcoal outline, not a second gold fill.
- Tabs, menus, and listboxes follow the APG: Tab moves between widgets, arrows move inside, Escape closes, Home and End move to the ends. `tabindex` is `0` or `-1` only.
- Covering menus and confirm dialogs trap focus. Set `inert` on the background. Move focus inside on open. Return focus to the trigger on close. `overscroll-behavior: contain` on the cover.
- Confirm dialogs do not close on scrim click. A language dropdown closes on outside pointer and on Escape.
- 44px minimum hit target on every control, including the menu icon, dropdown option, tab, and checkbox. Extend with padding when the visible mark is smaller. Hit areas do not overlap. This is a hit target, not a spacing token.
- Placeholders are examples. Every field has a visible label. Required mark is a charcoal asterisk after the label. Optional is the word optional in the label.
- Do not block paste.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no translate, no scale, no stagger. Dropdown and menu use a 150ms opacity crossfade, or none. Named properties only: `opacity`, `background-color`, `color`, `border-color`. Never `transition: all`. Easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`. High-frequency hover is 150ms or instant.
- Hover styles are inside `@media (hover: hover)`.
- Skip to content is the first focusable control on `/login`, ops, account, and maintenance.
- Page works at 320px without horizontal scroll, and at 200% zoom. No fixed height on a text container. Strings may grow. Do not set a fixed width on a button label.
- Dates shown anywhere this phase use `DD/MM/YYYY`, Monday week, Western numerals. The countdown is not a date.
- Prices already on Framer pages convert in place. `$` amounts are USD. AED amounts stay AED. Do not design a rate-unavailable message.
