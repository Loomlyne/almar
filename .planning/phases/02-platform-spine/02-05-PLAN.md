## Phase Goal

**As a** guest, **I want to** change language and currency on the page I am already on, **so that** the words and the prices follow me without a new URL.

<objective>
The live header gains currency and language. A pick stays on the same URL. Prices already written in the Framer HTML convert from a cached rate. A failed fetch is fixed in the build, not shown as a guest error.

Purpose: I18N-01 and I18N-02 are visible on the header she already uses, not a separate i18n layer.
Output: Header controls, cookies, FX cache, tests.
</objective>

Do not render a guest-facing failure. Do not add a retry button.

Prices in Framer HTML that this plan converts are amounts already marked with a data attribute the injector adds when it finds a currency amount. Do not invent new prices. If a page has no amount, the control still switches the cookie.
