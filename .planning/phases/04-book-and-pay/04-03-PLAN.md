## Phase Goal

**As** a guest, **I want** to go from a stay page to a clear booking flow with my dates already filled, **so that**
I can add what I want, enter who is travelling, and reach payment without retyping anything.

<objective>
Build `/booking/trip` in three languages as a static page that talks only to `/api/booking/quote` and
`/api/booking/hold` (04-02): Stay summary → Add-ons → Travellers → Pay, with reasons shown, the cart from the server's
numbers, the 30-minute hold and its countdown. Add "Book" on the stay page. Task 1 draws the screens the canvas does
not have, for the owner's signature, before any wiring.

Purpose: B-01, B-02, B-09…B-13, B-21, D-46…D-52. Output: the files above; the Payment Element is mounted by 04-06.
</objective>
