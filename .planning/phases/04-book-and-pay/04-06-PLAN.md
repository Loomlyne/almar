## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 (`**Mode:** mvp`), quoted as written: "A guest can request a real one-destination trip (stay +
add-ons), pay it by bank transfer, and the owner records the payment in the dashboard. No card provider is needed."
(Not in user-story form; the planner does not invent one, as 04-08 says. The controller may run `/gsd-mvp-phase 4`.)

This plan is the guest's last slice of that goal: after it, a guest presses Send request and sees what to pay, by when
and where the booking page is; a refresh never loses or doubles the request; a signed-in guest finds every booking at
`/bookings`. Each wiring task leaves the guest able to do something new: Task 3 send and resume, Task 4 the list.
