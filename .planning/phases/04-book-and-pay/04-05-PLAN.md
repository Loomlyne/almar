## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 4 (`**Mode:** mvp`), quoted as written: "A guest can request a real one-destination trip (stay +
add-ons), pay it by bank transfer, and the owner records the payment in the dashboard. No card provider is needed."
(The line is not in user-story form; the planner does not invent one, as 04-08 says. The controller may run
`/gsd-mvp-phase 4`.)

This plan is the guest's paper trail under that goal: after it, the guest is told how to pay (email and booking page),
gets a receipt when the owner records the money, is told when a request lapses, and has one page that always shows the
booking as it is; the owner gets a notice for each request and each recorded payment.

<objective>
Fill 04-08's email seam with six Resend templates (request received with transfer instructions, owner request notice,
payment receipt, owner payment notice, request lapsed, owner request-withdrawn notice) and build the guest booking page
`/bookings/trip?ref=&t=` (and `/ar`, `/es`) on 04-08's `POST /api/booking/view`, with the guest's Withdraw control for an
unpaid request on 04-08's `POST /api/booking/withdraw` (R2). Pictures of every email and page state are signed by the
owner before anything is wired.

Purpose: B-14 (receipt after recording, no PDF), B-15 (balance by transfer instructions), B-16 (booking page), B-18 (owner
on each request and each recorded payment), B-26 (details at once), B-28 (reply with the bank receipt), B-29 (all fees
paid by sender), B-30 (bank fields, note line in the guest's language), B-32/B-33/B-34 (part received, accepted as paid,
money after lapse or cancel, in words); the owner's 2026-10-20 answers: R2 (P-19 changed: the guest withdraws an unpaid
request on this page, the owner gets `request_withdrawn_owner`, the guest gets no email) and R1 (P-09 changed: a
part-paid request never lapses and keeps its transfer instructions after its deadline); PAY-10, PAY-17, PAY-18, PAY-20,
BOOK-08, OPS-10, OPS-13, AUTH-01. Output: the files above, 279 signed pictures, node and Playwright tests, and the owner
steps that make Resend send from `almar`, `almar-preview` and `almar-ops`.
</objective>
