### C. Status rule (`booking_next_status`; record and void both use it; 04-09's preview mirrors it)

| Status in | settled >= grand | plan deposit and settled >= deposit | otherwise |
|-----------|------------------|-------------------------------------|-----------|
| awaiting_payment | paid_in_full | deposit_paid | awaiting_payment ("part received" when paid > 0; it then never lapses: P-09) |
| deposit_paid | paid_in_full | deposit_paid | awaiting_payment (void only; fresh deadline) |
| paid_in_full | paid_in_full | deposit_paid | awaiting_payment (void only; fresh deadline) |
| held, confirmed, completed, expired, cancelled | unchanged | unchanged | unchanged |

Record on expired / cancelled: money kept, status unchanged, flag paid_after_expiry / paid_after_cancel, nights never
re-claimed (B-34). Record above grand: flag overpaid. Accept as paid (B-33): awaiting_payment, deposit_paid, confirmed,
completed only; gap = target - settled after the row; written off on that row. Void of confirmed / completed below the
deposit floor, on every plan (`booking_deposit_floor`: the stored deposit_fils for a deposit plan, `(grand_fils ×
deposit_bp + 5000) / 10000` for a full plan; P-21 as the owner changed it 2026-10-20): `cancel_first`. Nights:
deposit_paid / paid_in_full / confirmed / completed keep them for good; an unpaid awaiting_payment request holds them
to `hold_expires_at` (the deadline) and lapses after it; from its first recorded payment a request keeps them for good
(P-09): past its deadline it stays awaiting_payment, flagged `deadline_passed_part_paid`, until the owner records more,
Cancels (the flag then stays until Resolve: money is still held and refunds are outside the site, B-31) or resolves it.
A void that leaves no payment on an awaiting request times its nights again to `greatest(deadline, now() + window)`.
The guest may withdraw an unpaid request (P-19): cancelled, its nights freed at once.
