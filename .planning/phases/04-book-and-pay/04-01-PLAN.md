## Phase Goal

**As** a guest, **I want** the price of my stay and add-ons computed the same way every time, **so that** what I
pay, what ALMAR receives and what the booking stores always agree.

<objective>
Write the money engine as pure TypeScript in `lib/money/`: integer fils, one rounding rule, the booking price in
the order the owner signed (B-05), deposit or full (PAY-03), balance and its due date (B-15), and the late-booking
rule (B-21). No database, no network, no React. 04-02 feeds it live rates from the database; 04-03 and 04-05 only
format its numbers. The server is the only place that calls `priceBooking` for money that is charged.

Purpose: B-05, B-06, B-21, PAY-01/02/03. Output: four modules and one node test file, all green.
</objective>
