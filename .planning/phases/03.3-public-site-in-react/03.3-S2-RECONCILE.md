**S2-26 Fifth plan check: test helpers and job 11's real shapes.**

Moving the pointer away:
- One helper, `pointerOff(page)`, used in plans 13 and 17. It moves the pointer to a point where
  `document.elementFromPoint(x, y)?.closest('[aria-roledescription="carousel"]')` is null, for example the top
  left of the on-image header, which is a sibling of the hero. It asserts that, then blurs the active element.
- It runs right after every `goto` and after every Pause, Play or dot click, before any wait. The hero is full
  height, so no fixed coordinate is safe.

Reveal checks:
- Opacity and translate are read on `el.closest('[data-reveal]')`: the `li[data-reveal=row]` and the hero
  overlay `[data-reveal=headline]`. They are never read on the card or the `<h1>` inside.
- "Revealed" means: after `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded`, `expect.poll` (up to 3000 ms) until opacity is `"1"` and
  translate is `"none"`, on a load without `page.clock.install()`. Job 11's row reveal runs 1.18 s.
- Reduced motion: opacity `"1"` and translate `"none"` at once on every `[data-reveal]` element.

Grid: `grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-4 md:gap-8`. Every `li` is `md:col-span-2` and the fifth is
`md:col-start-2`, so the centred card keeps one column's width and the column gap stays 32 px.

Slider call:
- `<Slider layout="hero" autoplay images={hero.map(({ url, alt }) => ({ src: url, alt }))} labels={…} />`.
  Job 11's `autoplay` defaults to false, and its `SliderImage` is `{src, alt}`.
- Slides 2 and 3 may load lazily. Image checks poll `complete && naturalWidth > 0` for at least
  3 × `AUTOPLAY_MS`, or hero mode loads its three slides eagerly in case B.

Card unchanged-proof: before editing `card.tsx`, the executor captures job 11's default `PortraitCard` render
as a string, stores it in the test and compares against it.

Served HTML: no `style` attribute with opacity, translate or transform, and no unscoped `opacity-0` or
`translate-*` class on a `[data-reveal]` element. The `pre-reveal:` classes are allowed.

Dot stop-for-good: plan 13 keeps "stays on 2 over `AUTOPLAY_MS` + 1 s" after a verified `pointerOff`. It is
the only proof of S2-22's rule. Plan 17 notes that plan 13 proves it.

Test port: `PW_PORT=${PW_PORT:-3048}` in plan 17's verify.
