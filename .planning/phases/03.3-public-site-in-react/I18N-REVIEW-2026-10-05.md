## Rules applied

1. **Brand.** Latin `ALMAR` in every Arabic and Spanish string, including running Arabic prose
   ("تخطّط ALMAR", "مجلة ALMAR", "مرحباً ALMAR"). The Arabic rendering "المار" is kept only for the full name
   *ALMAR Private Journeys*, exactly as the signed footer line already writes it ("المار للرحلات الخاصة"):
   footer copyright, wordmark alt, the welcome letter's signer and the About statement. Why: the newest
   signed pictures (S3 About/Contact) and the controller's brief use Latin `ALMAR`; the older Sept-29 canvas
   dictionary (`lib/copy/framer-source.ts`, `Hero.dc.html`) used "المار" in prose, but none of those lines
   is rendered by the React site any more, and one site needs one rule.
2. **Arabic register.** Modern Standard Arabic, two address forms, one per surface, matching what was signed:
   - controls of the planner, calendar, filters and sliders: singular masculine imperative
     (canvas-signed "خطّط لرحلتك", "اختر وجهة", "أضف ضيفًا");
   - editorial sentences and section calls to action: plural of respect ("رحلتكم", "شاركونا", "ابدأوا رحلتكم",
     the signed About/Contact lines).
   Within-file mismatches were fixed (stay page "اطلب عبر واتساب" next to "اطلبوا…" → "اطلبوا عبر واتساب";
   experiences meta "لك ولمجموعتك" → "لكم ولمجموعتكم"; the blog's planner region name now equals the home's).
   Calques removed ("حولكم" for *around you* → "خصيصاً لكم"; "القفازات البيضاء"; "الباسيفيكية" → "المحيط
   الهادئ"; "المتحف الذهبي" → "متحف الذهب"). Arabic comma "،" and "؟" throughout; em dashes inside Arabic prose
   replaced by "،" or ":" (the "—" between a stay name and "معرض N" in alt texts is kept, it mirrors EN).
3. **Spanish register.** *tú* everywhere. The signed strings all use *tú* (journey bar D-08 "Planea tu viaje",
   canvas "Planifica tu viaje", "Ponte en contacto", board k16 "Tu estancia…"); the drafts mixed *ustedes*
   (home sections, blog, letter, tiers, stays) and *usted* (catalogue). Neutral Latin-American lexicon:
   *agregar* not *añadir*, *estancia* not *estadía*, *región cafetera*, *a solicitud* not *bajo petición*.
4. **Place names.** One Arabic spelling per place, see the table. `ميديين` (stays list meta) → `ميديلين`;
   `ساليتنو` → `سالينتو`.
5. **Digits.** Western digits in Arabic, as the site does by design; counts below ten in Arabic prose are
   written as words where grammar asks for it ("حمّامان", "سريران كوين").
6. **Keys, slots, plural categories, HTML.** Unchanged. Every `{slot}`, `#`, `[RATE]` and plural key set is
   as before; the key-tree tests still pass.
7. **Alt texts.** Describe what EN describes. Stay hero alts now mirror the EN "— hero" pattern in all
   three languages (the Getsemaní and Historic Center heroes carried the old home-card alt instead); the
   concierge category was missing from two alts; decorative alts stay `""`.
8. **Status.** Every AR and ES row that was `draft` is `published` after review (480 fixture rows, the
   destinations page copy and the 404 copy). EN rows untouched, including the EN rows that are themselves
   `draft` (gallery strip alts 6–13, "Begin Your Journey" still, two catalogue alts).
9. **Signed and pinned strings.** Kept as written unless a clear error. The one pinned AR string changed is
   the WhatsApp greeting (brand rule, it was a flagged draft, not a signed picture); listed below with the
   tests updated.
