Arabic and Spanish: 14 pages become 42. Eight plans in four waves. Executors run on Sonnet 5.5, the lead and
reviewers on Opus 5.5. Overlaps between plans are settled in `03.3-RECONCILE.md`.

## What you can click on the preview

- **Language:** the address changes (`/ar/private-stays`), and Arabic is right-to-left from the first moment.
- **Currency:** AED, USD or EUR in the header moves the three journey prices on the home page. The choice is
  remembered, including after a language switch, and the page prints the date of the exchange rates.
- **Journey bar:** Where → When → Who works on the home page, and on each stay page with that stay locked.
- **`/private-stays`:** filters, search, the result count and an empty state.
- **Stay pages:** the photo gallery, the fact strip (guests, bedrooms, bathrooms, beds, neighbourhood),
  blocked dates on the calendar, related stays, and "Request Inquiry", which goes to the contact page.

## Eight controls not shown in this slice

Each appears only when it can really work: **the journey bar's Search button**, the **cart**, **Login**,
the **footer newsletter**, **"List with us"**, the stay page's **"Add" buttons**, the stay page's
**"Continue"**, and **"See Packages"**.

## What waits on you

1. **Enable R2** in the Cloudflare dashboard (account "Almar Private Journey"). Your preview review cannot
   start until the images are on R2.
2. Your word on the bucket `almar-media` and its address `media.almarprivatejourney.com`.
3. **Real nightly rates and minimum stays** for the 12 stays. Until then the stay pages show no amount and
   no minimum; nothing is invented.
4. **The hero video file.** It is still served from `files.catbox.moe`.
5. **Your review of the draft Arabic and Spanish:** the 12 stays, the 404 page, and the new line
   "Converted at the exchange rate of {date}."
