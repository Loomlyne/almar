## 5. What the two forms do

**Contact: `POST /api/contact`**
- **Order:** Origin same host, else 403 → `application/x-www-form-urlencoded` only, else 415 → body above 16,384 bytes
  is 413 → rate limit, 429 → any of the 11 honeypots filled is 400 → field checks are 400 with error codes → no key
  is 503 → Resend failed is 502 → sent is 200 `{"ok":true}`.
- **Fields:** `Name` (≤100), `Phone` (≤40, optional), `Email` (≤254), `Journey Type` (one of `Experiences`,
  `Services`, `Full Journey`, `Group Event`, `General Question`), `Journey Details` (≤4000), and `Locale`.
- **The email:** exactly one plain-text email, from and to `inquiries@almarprivatejourney.com`, reply-to the
  visitor. Subject `Website inquiry · <journey type> · <name>`, for example
  `Website inquiry · General Question · Ana test`. The body lists Name, Phone / WhatsApp, Email, Journey type,
  Language, Page and the message. No auto-reply.
- **What the visitor sees:**
  - sending: the busy button `Sending…`;
  - sent: `Your message was sent.` replaces the whole box;
  - any other outcome: `It did not send. Write to inquiries@almarprivatejourney.com or message us on WhatsApp.`,
    with working links, and the fields keep their values;
  - field errors: one under each field, and focus moves to the first bad field.
- **With JavaScript off:** no box and no `Start Your Inquiry`; the details' WhatsApp row still works.

**Newsletter: `POST /newsletter`**
- **Request:** multipart `Email` plus the 11 honeypots.
- **Order:** Origin 403 → multipart only, else 415 → body above 8 KiB is 413 → rate limit, 429 → honeypot or bad
  address is 400 → no key is 503 → `contacts.create({ email })` failed is 502 → 200 `{"ok":true}` with no contact id.
- No email is sent. GET and HEAD answer 405. `/ar/newsletter`, `/es/newsletter` and `/newsletter/x` stay the static 404.
- **What the visitor sees:**
  - success: the toast `You're on the list.`, which replaces the untrue "Check your inbox";
  - failure, timeout after 20 s included: `You were not added. Please try again, or write to inquiries@almarprivatejourney.com.`;
  - a bad address: `Enter an email as name@example.com.`
- With JavaScript off there is no box.
