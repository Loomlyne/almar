## Deviations from the plan

1. **[Content] Seven experiences, not five.** The plan says "5 experiences". The 12 published stay pages
   publish seven distinct ones: welcome-cocktail, cartagena-night-experience, cartagena-heritage-tours,
   rosario-islands-escape, yacht-island-charters, medellin-renaissance, medellin-discovery-tours. All seven
   are in the fixture (a stay lists three of them). Plan 05/06 tests that assume five should read seven.
2. **[Plan] Acceptance grep too loose.** `grep -cE "_ar|_es|translations" lib/data/types.ts` is 1, not 0:
   the design-mandated field `price_estimate` contains `_es`. The boundary form of the rule
   (`\b[a-z]+_(en|ar|es)\b` and the identifier `translations`) is 0 and is what `data-boundary.test.mjs`
   enforces. No type carries a language suffix or a translations member.
3. **[Addition] `lib/data/fixtures/team-translations.json` (`[]`).** One more fixture than the plan's ten,
   so `getTeam` follows the same one-record-per-language shape as the other entities when a member is first
   published. Empty; adds no content.
4. **[Plan] `StayFilter` lives in `lib/data/types.ts`** (the artifacts list says so) and is re-exported
   from `stays.ts` as the design shows. `parseStayQuery` takes an optional second argument
   `{ destinations }` so a caller can reject a slug that is not a real destination, since the pure module
   cannot know them.
5. **[R-7 shape]** `HomeStory` carries `is_published` as well as the fields plan 04 lists, for the same
   reason the other rows do. Plan 04 can ignore it.
6. **[Design] `bathrooms` is null for "8, 1 social"** (Bocagrande), following the plan's "numeric only when a
   plain count". Design 3.9's prose says 8; the type comment and the plan say null. Null chosen; the
   published text stays in `bathrooms_label`.
7. **[Manifest] Top-level shape is an array** of `{ key, source, source_kind, used_by }`. `used_by` holds
   the entity row id (stays, destinations, catalogue, tiers, stories) or the block label `home:hero`,
   `home:welcome`, `home:gallery`. Keys end `.webp` for every source, including the 66 Framer JPEG sources,
   as plan 07 expects (it negotiates WebP on fetch). Sources keep the `?width=&height=` query the live page
   uses, which is also where the fixture width and height come from; local sizes are read from the WebP
   header.

## Flagged for the owner (content, not code)

- **AR and ES are drafts** of the published English, status `draft`, everywhere. AR is Gulf-friendly MSA,
  ES neutral Latin American. The three home stays and the home hero, welcome letter, tier names and
  tier images reuse the strings already in `lib/copy/home.ts`; everything else is newly drafted and needs
  his review. Property and neighbourhood proper nouns keep their Spanish spelling in AR and ES text.
- **Two stays publish "-" placeholders in their layout line** (`private-island-cartagena`:
  "— bathrooms, — beds"; `private-island-estate-cartagena`: "— beds"). Carried verbatim in `description`
  because the rule is published content verbatim. Those two stays also have no published Bathrooms or Beds
  stat, so `bathrooms_label` / `beds_label` are null where unpublished. A one-line fix once he supplies the
  counts.
- **Typos kept in English** as the plan says ("Private Pool: Pivada", "2 queen beds beds"). The AR and ES
  drafts render the intended word.
- **Destination region** ("Caribbean coast", "Andes") comes from the signed Hero board, not from a
  published page; `short_line` is null (D-39: the owner writes it). Both are the one place a destination
  field is not read off a live page.
- **Alt text that was not published**: ten of the 15 home gallery images, `luxury-ground-transport` and
  `cartagena-night-experience` have an empty alt on the live page. They carry the derived alt
  "Colombia, Beautifully Captured — N" or the item name, with English status `draft` so the owner can find
  them. Stay hero and gallery alts follow the live "<Title> — hero / gallery N" pattern.
- **Home gallery order** is document order on the live page: the five images that have alt text first, then
  the ten that do not. The live page does not make the intended order recoverable.
- **Welcome letter has three paragraphs** on the live page (the design text said four); three are carried.
- **The three home stays have destination `cartagena`; `santa-fe-farm-antioquia` and
  `sopetran-country-estate` are `medellin`** (design 7.4, still unconfirmed by the owner). Range stays:
  `private-island-cartagena` min 16 / max 20, `private-island-estate-cartagena` min 20 / max 30 (design
  7.5, unconfirmed).
- The live stay pages also publish 24 to 36 "amenity N" photos per stay. They are not in the design and
  not carried.
