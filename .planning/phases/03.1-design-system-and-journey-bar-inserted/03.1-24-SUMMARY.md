## Deviations from Plan

- [Rule 3] `SearchIcon` added to components/icons/icons.tsx (no search glyph existed).
- [D-70 over plan text] The guest picker is centred under the Guests tab, not end-aligned (plan truth said end-aligned; D-70 and the UI-SPEC bullet win).
- [D-62/D-63, from caller] JourneyBar gains optional `lockDestination` (Destination segment shown, aria-disabled, no popup) and `blockedDates` (passed to DateRangePanel). DateRangePanel gained an optional `blocked` prop: blocked days are aria-disabled and "unavailable", cannot be picked, and a range that would cross one restarts at the clicked day.
- Extra optional props: `sentinelRef` (docked), `onEditSearch` (summary), `initialOpen` / `forceMissing` (harness). `forceMissing` only starts the bar in the attempted state; Missing is then derived from the value.
- Summary `Edit search` is a ghost Button in a `border-s` cell; the spec does not add nights anywhere but summary.
- Outside clicks inside the bar are not dismissals; segments toggle/switch panels themselves. Switching panels suppresses focus return so focus is not stolen back.
- Spec: Next's route announcer is also role=alert, so alerts are located as `p[role=alert]`. The outside-click test retries until Radix arms its listener.
- Scene wrappers use `mx-auto px-6` so the panels' 16px collision padding does not shift them at the viewport edge.
