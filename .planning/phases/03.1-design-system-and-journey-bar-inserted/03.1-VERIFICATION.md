## Gaps

1. **Phone type sizes are outside the token source.** `app/globals.css:126-128` (`--text-hero: 40px`, `--text-display: 32px`, `--text-heading: 24px`), restated at `:133-135`. D-20 asks for these as token values per breakpoint. They are hand-written, not in `tokens.json`. The guardrail does not see them. Why it matters: SC1 says no px font sizes outside the token source, and in Phase 5 Settings › Brand (DSGN-04) would override only the desktop values. Fix: add a phone size per step to `tokens.json` and generate this block.
2. **Brand hexes copied into the Settings screen.** `app/dashboard/(ops)/settings/settings-screen.tsx:12-22` lists 9 hex literals. They match the tokens today, so the test passes. Why it matters: a second copy will drift when tokens change, and it is the screen DSGN-04 will make editable. Fix: read them from `tokens.json` or from the CSS variables.
3. **Physical utility the guardrail misses.** `app/dashboard/(ops)/layout.tsx:217` `object-left`. In Arabic the logo stays pinned to the left. The test bans `left-`, not `object-left`. Fix: add `rtl:object-right` (or drop the alignment), and add `object-(left|right)` to the guardrail.
4. **English-only strings inside shared controls.** `components/ui/field.tsx:166` ("Hide password" / "Show password") and `:70` ("Apply"). On an Arabic or Spanish page the eye button's accessible name stays English. Why it matters: DSGN-03/05 and the EN/AR/ES rule. Fix: take labels as props or from `lib/copy/`.
5. **Canvas page titles still say "for your sign-off".** In `project/canvas.json`, the notes `head-found`, `head-comp`, `head-journey`, `head-public`, `head-guest`, `head-dashboard` all read "… · for your sign-off · …". No `project/README.md` is published in the served version. So the only record of approval is UAT step 1 and the plan summaries. Why it matters: anyone who opens the canvas sees six pages still waiting. Fix: change the six titles to "approved" (owner's call).
6. **Dead marker class names.** `components/ui/field.tsx:61,69,77` (`field-coupon`, `ui-button-inline`, `field-error`, `field-hint`) and `:153` (`field-control is-password`) have no CSS behind them. Harmless leftovers from the old stylesheet. Remove them if no test selects them.
