## What it does

- **Team** (canvas DashTeamLegal, Team half; kit editor page, option B). The list shows each member's name, role and
  Shown status. Members reorder with Move up/down or by dragging. Team starts empty with "No team members yet" and New
  member; no member exists until the owner adds one (D-57).
- **Team editor.** Text holds Name, Role and Bio in English only; Arabic and Spanish are added on Save. Photo is
  optional. Social media holds rows of Platform (Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, X) plus an https address, up to eight.
  Email is optional with the line "Not shown on the public site." Delete member is off while the member is published.
- **Packages.** The three home journeys (C-15): reorder only, with no New, no Delete and no booking. Show and hide are
  the kit's Publish and Unpublish.
- **Journey editor.** Text holds Name, Tagline, Duration line, Ideal-for label, Ideal for and Description. Photo is
  required. Price holds the Price line, the owner's own text stored as typed, plus how the site reads the saved line.
- Copy is in `lib/copy/ops-team.ts`, EN/AR/ES. Canvas lines are verbatim, except dashboard Spanish is usted. Every other
  line is a draft for the owner.

## What changed after the signature

- **Wiring.** `TeamScreen` and `JourneysScreen` read `/api/ops/team` and `/api/ops/journeys` and save through
  `saveThenPublish`: one save, then the publish call on Publish. Unpublish goes to `/api/ops/publish`. Delete and reorder
  are on Team; Packages has reorder only. The old `content-screen.tsx` is deleted. `/content/team` and
  `/catalog/packages` are in `OPS_LIVE_SECTIONS` (`lib/ops-routes.ts`).
- **Team save** (`teamPayload`, `team-forms.ts`):
  - the web address is sent as typed until the member is published, never after;
  - an empty email is sent as null;
  - links are the trimmed rows that have an address;
  - the http link, slug and email rules are checked before any request.
- **Journey save** (`journeyPayload`, `journey-forms.ts`):
  - sends `{ id, is_featured, media_id, translations }`;
  - no slug, no price column;
  - an Arabic or Spanish record with no price line is left out, because the server requires one in every record.
- **Kit:** Publish is off whenever a save is needed and the screen holds Save (`c4155f9`). A screen adds nothing to
  "Publish needs:".
