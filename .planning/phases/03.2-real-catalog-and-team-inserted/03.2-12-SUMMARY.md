# 03.2-12 Summary: Coming soon pages, Content rail, harness gate

Content -> Pages, Blog, Legal and Navigation and footer now show a Coming soon page with no control on it. The old
drawers with dead Publish buttons are gone. The rail marks those four with a Soon chip.

## What shipped

- `ComingSoonScreen({ kind })` and `SoonChip` in `coming-soon.tsx`. No button, input, link or onClick. Locale comes from the `almar-locale` cookie.
- `pages`, `blog` and `legal` pages render it. `navigation/page.tsx` is new. All four are `robots: noindex`.
- `ops-shell.tsx`: Content rail lists Pages, Blog, Team, Legal, Media, Navigation and footer. Soon chip and accessible name "<label>, soon" on Pages, Blog, Legal, Navigation. None on Packages, Team, Media. The dead `<Sidebar open={false}>` is removed.
- `OpsShell mode="ops"` now renders its children. Before, it showed "Not ready." for every section, so the Media screen (03.2-09) would also have been invisible on the ops host.
- `OPS_LIVE_SECTIONS` gained `/content/pages`, `/content/blog`, `/content/legal`, `/content/navigation` (controller request, follow-up commit 2026-10-13).
- Phone drawer: the shell measures the header with a `ResizeObserver` into `--ops-header-h`; the open drawer pads by `calc(var(--ops-header-h, 5rem) + 0.5rem)`. On a phone in ops mode the header is about 132px, not 78 (Grok's finding on PR #12, fixed 2026-10-14).
- `harness-client.tsx` imports fixtures and scenes only inside `if (process.env.NODE_ENV !== "production")`. A production build holds no harness scene and no fixture (3.1 leftover 5).
- Copy in `lib/copy/ops-content.ts`, EN/AR/ES. Pages and Blog use the canvas words verbatim. Legal and Navigation are two drafts the owner accepted.
- Pictures: owner signed at `d5339ae` (all four Soon chips, Legal and Navigation drafts in EN/AR/ES, 20px phone title, drawer fix). Folder `pictures/03.2-12/`.
