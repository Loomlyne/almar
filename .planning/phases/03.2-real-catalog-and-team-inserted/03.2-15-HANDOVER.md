## Migration `20261019140000_stay_access_notes_i18n.sql`
- Renamed from `20261015120000` on 2026-10-19 (controller; `git mv`, content unchanged): live already has
  `20261019120000_owner_emails`, so the old number would have run out of order.
- `alter table public.stay_access add column notes_ar text check (char_length(notes_ar) <= 2000), add column notes_es
  text check (char_length(notes_es) <= 2000);` and `create or replace function public.ops_save_stay_access(p jsonb)`
  with the same signature, `search_path = ''` and security; it only adds the two notes (absent = untouched, empty =
  null). Ends with the revoke from public/anon/authenticated and grant to service_role (repeats the ACL already in
  place; kept on the controller's word).
- **Safe on live rows:** additive. Two nullable columns, no default, no backfill, no rewrite; existing rows read null
  for both. No grant, policy or view: `stay_access` stays closed to anon and authenticated (C-14). No existing row is
  changed.
- **Order at landing:** apply it live **before** deploying `almar-ops`. The new GET selects `notes_ar, notes_es`; on a
  database without the columns the Access card would fail to load.
- Not applied anywhere but local stacks. The controller applies it at landing after a second-model check.

## Owner's test steps (after landing and the migration, signed in on the dashboard)
1. Open Catalog → Stays, open a stay. Expected: below its other cards, "Rates" and "Availability" on the left, "Access"
   on the right (one column on a phone).
2. In Rates, leave Price per night empty. Expected: "No nightly rate yet: guests cannot book this stay."
3. Type your price per night in AED, click "Save price". Expected: "Saved"; the warning is gone.
4. Type a price with a comma or three decimals, click "Save price". Expected: "Type the amount in AED, with up to 2
   decimals and a dot, for example 1250.50."; nothing saved.
5. Click "Add range", choose the first and last night, type a price, "Save range". Expected: the range is listed with its
   nights and price.
6. Add a second range of the same length over one of those nights. Expected: "Another range of the same length already
   covers these nights."; not saved.
7. Under "Check prices", choose nights across your range and outside it, click "Check". Expected: one line per night,
   "Range" price inside the range, "Base" price outside.
8. Delete the range. Expected: "Delete this date range?"; Delete removes it and its nights go back to the base price.
9. In Availability, move to a month with a block from the Calendar. Expected: those days marked "Blocked" and the reason
   listed under "Blocked days this month"; "Block dates for this stay" opens the Calendar.
10. In Access, type the address, Wi-Fi name and password, door code and Notes in English, click "Save access".
    Expected: "Saved"; "Edit translations" shows the Notes in Arabic and Spanish, each box as wide as Notes.
11. Change the Notes, then press Escape (or Back). Expected: "Discard changes?"; "Keep editing" keeps your text.
12. Switch the dashboard to Arabic. Expected: the page mirrors, all text in Arabic; then Spanish, all text in Spanish.
13. Open the public stay page signed out. Expected: no access detail (address, Wi-Fi, door code, notes) anywhere on it.
