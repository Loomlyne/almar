## What shipped

- **Rates card:** price per night in AED, "Save price", date ranges (add, edit, delete), and "Check prices" listing each night's price and source (range, base, no rate) from the existing stay-rates route. No new arithmetic; amounts are format-checked and sent as text. No rate value exists in code, seed or picture; the owner types every rate.
- **Availability card:** month grid of blocked days for this stay, reasons list, and a link "Block dates for this stay" to the Calendar (`?block=stay&stay_id=`).
- **Access card:** address, Wi-Fi name, Wi-Fi password, door code, and Notes. Notes are written in English; Arabic and Spanish are filled by the AI on Save (kit auto-translate, "Edit translations" link, no tabs). If the AI fails, the card says so and Save access stays enabled for a retry.
- Wiring: one `...stayTabSections(...)` spread at the end of `sections` in `stay-editor.tsx`; each card reports its own dirty flag, OR-ed into the page's `dirty`, so Escape or Back asks "Discard changes?". The dirty report runs from a layout effect (`004401c`) after a load-timing failure.
- Two "Edit translations" controls on a saved stay (Text card, Access card) have different accessible names, built from signed strings.
- Round-3 items for PR #19 done here on the controller's word (`887f913`): New stay and Close clear the list alert; the list wrapper stays mounted so focus stays.
