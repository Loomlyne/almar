## Found, not fixed
1. **Kit Back guard races a navigation after Save.** After Save closes the page, the guard's cleanup runs
   `history.back()` to drop its extra entry; a navigation started right away (a `page.goto` in the test) was aborted
   (`net::ERR_ABORTED`). Kit behaviour, `components/ops/edit-page.tsx`; the fix belongs to the follow-ups job, item 2.
   The test waiting for the page to close is a workaround, not the fix.
2. **Pick list close → date picker dismiss (minor).** On a phone, opening the Dates picker right after choosing a
   destination or stay in the pick list can close the picker again: the Select hands focus back to its button as it
   closes, and the Popover takes that as focus outside. A fast second tap can close the picker; a second tap opens it.
   Shared `components/ui/select.tsx` / `components/ops/date-range-field.tsx`; not changed here.
