## What changed
- `app/dashboard/(ops)/catalog/catalog-forms.ts` (pure): form types, field lists, `liveFields`, list helpers
  (`groupAmount`, `filterCatalogRows`), and the wiring helpers `destinationFormOf`, `catalogItemFormOf`,
  `withTranslations`, `sameForm`, `normalisePrice` ("" -> null, "1250" -> "1250.00", "12.5" -> "12.50", anything
  else refused), `destinationPayload`, `catalogItemPayload` (refuses a bad price with `field: "price_aed"`),
  `connectionChanges`, `movedIds`. English is always sent published; Arabic and Spanish go as draft on Save unless that
  language is already published, and as published on Publish. Money stays a two-decimal AED string, never a float.
- Views (props only): `destinations/destinations-view.tsx`, `experiences/catalog-items-view.tsx`, both on the kit
  `EditPage` (option B): full page with a back link, outlined Save, teal Publish, sections as cards.
  - Destination: Text, Photo (hero + inset), Connections ("Experiences and services") in the main column; Settings (web
    address) and Stays (`Stays (n)`, read-only list) on the side. A new destination shows Text, Photo and Settings only.
    Delete is disabled while it has stays ("Move its stays first").
  - Item: Text, Photo, Connections in the main column; Price (Unit, Price in AED) and Settings (type chips, UAE-side
    service, "Home pickup · pre-selected at checkout" with its hint and refusal line, web address) on the side. The
    refusals for home pickup, price and a taken web address show on their own field.
  - `catalog/locked-address.tsx`: a published item's web address is text with the line under it, not a read-only box.
- Screens (wired): `destinations/destinations-screen.tsx` and `experiences/catalog-items-screen.tsx` call the owner API
  (`/api/ops/destinations`, `/api/ops/catalog`, `/api/ops/stays`, `/api/ops/publish`): list, open, Save, Publish (save
  then publish, in that order), Unpublish, Delete, reorder (drag and the move buttons), connect/remove items for a
  destination (one catalog save per changed item, stops at the first refusal, then re-reads the list).
- Stay filter (controller's option A): picking a stay makes **one** `GET /api/ops/stays?id=` and keeps its
  `experience_ids` + `service_ids` for the session. While that call runs the list has `aria-busy`, the select ignores a
  new pick and the rows stay as they are. If it fails, the list's error line shows and the filter stays on "All stays".
- `lib/ops-routes.ts`: `/catalog/destinations` and `/catalog/experiences` added to `OPS_LIVE_SECTIONS`.
- `lib/copy/ops-catalog.ts`: the `panel` group and the keys `tabDetails`, `tabExperiences`, `tabConnections`,
  `deleteNeedsUnpublish` removed (the kit holds them); `tabStays` kept as the Stays card title. No new copy since the
  signature. EN/AR/ES drafts, dashboard Spanish in usted; no prices, no legal wording, no people.
- Tests: `tests/catalog-forms.test.mjs` (18 node tests), `tests/helpers/ops-catalog-api.ts` (mocked owner API with a
  store), `tests/helpers/ops-catalog-pictures.ts`, scenes `ops-destinations.tsx` / `ops-catalog-items.tsx`, behaviour
  blocks in `tests/ops-destinations.spec.ts` and `tests/ops-catalog-items.spec.ts` (desktop EN and phone AR). The old
  panel-options scene and spec are removed.
