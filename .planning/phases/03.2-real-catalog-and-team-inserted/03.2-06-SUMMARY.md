# 03.2-06 Summary: Destinations and Experiences and services on the kit editor

Source: `03.2-06-PLAN.md`, `03.2-06-HANDOVER.md`, commit `fa5c3b2`. No migration. Not deployed.

## What shipped

- Destinations and Experiences and services pages on the kit `EditPage` (option B): list, open, Save, Publish
  (save first, then publish), Unpublish, Delete, reorder (drag and move buttons).
- Destination editor: Text, Photo, Connections to items; Settings (web address); read-only Stays card. Delete is off
  while the destination has stays.
- Item editor: Text, Photo, Connections; Price (unit, AED), type chips, UAE-side service, "Home pickup" flag, web address.
  Price is a two-decimal AED string, never a float. A published item's address is shown as text, not a box.
- Stay filter on the items list: one `GET /api/ops/stays?id=` per pick; the list is `aria-busy` while it runs.
- `/catalog/destinations` and `/catalog/experiences` added to `OPS_LIVE_SECTIONS` (`lib/ops-routes.ts`).
- Owner signed the editor pictures at 02:08 on 2026-10-15 (63 PNGs: 7 states x 390/834/1440 x EN/AR/ES; `2fc4126`).

## Deviations from the plan

1. Views are named `-view` (props only) and screens `-screen` (wired). `tests/phase-03-catalog.test.mjs` follows the screen import.
2. Tests are `tests/catalog-forms.test.mjs`, not the planned `tests/ops-catalog-forms.test.mjs` (hand-over lists the former).
3. Kit panel and tab keys were removed from `lib/copy/ops-catalog.ts` (the kit holds them).
4. Added after review (PR #18, 4 rounds, reviewer agent Sonnet 5.5; Grok 4.7 was unavailable in Claude Code):
   - a published item keeps what Publish needs (`liveGaps`), and removing a published item's only destination is refused (`strandedItems`);
   - connect loop reads the list first and says "n of m saved" if it stops;
   - one Save/Publish/connect/move at a time (ref guard); focus stays on the moved row after reorder (`focusMovedRow`; the disabled fieldset was dropped);
   - price shown invalid as typed; `normalisePrice` caps at 10 whole digits like the server;
   - new EN/AR/ES draft strings: `liveNeeds`, `validPrice`, `priceHint`, `lastDestination`, `connectPartial`.
5. `warmDashboard` test helper added so two workers do not compile the page cold together.
6. `catalog-screen.tsx` kept; stays and packages still imported it at this point.
