# 03.2-13 Summary: Calendar, block dates on the kit editor page

The owner blocks full days for everything, one destination or one stay, with an optional reason. Blocked days show on
the month grid. The dead New booking form is gone. STAY-04 is partial: ops blocks only (paid and held nights are Phase 4).

## What shipped

- `calendar-screen.tsx` loads `/api/ops/blocks` for the shown month and filter, adds and deletes through the same route, and refetches after each write. Month grid is in Dubai days, Monday first. Past days cannot be clicked.
- Block dates page on the kit `EditPage` (option B, signed 2026-10-15 at `cf8ea20`): a "Calendar" back link; cards "What to block" and "Dates" on the wide column, "Reason (optional)" on the narrow one; one column below 1024px. Save is the one action; a block has no publish step. The reason is the owner's note, not on the site, so nothing is translated.
- `block-form-fields.tsx` (`useBlockForm`, three field parts) and `calendar-view.tsx` (`BlockEditor`). `block-form.ts` is pure: form to contract 5.4 payload, query prefill parse (`?block=stay&stay_id=`).
- `lib/ops-routes.ts`: `/calendar` added to `OPS_LIVE_SECTIONS`.
- `components/ui/calendar.tsx` gains an optional `now`; `components/ops/date-range-field.tsx` passes the Dubai day. Other callers unchanged.
- PR #17 review fixes (reviewer agent, "ship with fixes", 8 items; commits `fa3430d`, `10a1aed`, `58b00f5`, specs `1591033`, `183d45d`): refused Delete keeps its own error; failed destinations or stays load keeps its own error; failed month load clears blocks and says why; Dubai day re-read on focus; the date field opens on the month of its first day (`CalendarPanel` optional `openOnStart`); a field change clears the last refusal; server `invalid` on `block` shows the generic sentence; unused copy removed. The view's `error` prop became `errors: string[]`.
