## Phase Goal

The owner makes a booking by hand (signed list item 7, OPS-06): it holds the nights until his "hold until" and emails
the guest a link to pay in the same branded checkout.

<objective>
Build New booking (canvas `DashNewBookingA` steps 1–3 and `DashNewBookingB` steps 4–5, D-84 subset) on
`dashboard.almarprivatejourney.com/bookings/new`, wired to owner-only endpoints that call Phase 4's functions:

- Guest: name, email, phone, language (EN / AR / ES). Customer search is not rendered (customers are v1.1).
- Stay and dates: stay (3.2's `GET /api/ops/stays`, bookable ones selectable, grouped by destination), arrive / leave
  with an availability calendar (Blocked, Booked), adults / children / infants.
- Extras: the offers 04-02's `booking_context` returns for that stay, with quantity rules by unit; pickup address when
  04-02's `needsPickupAddress` says so (B-11).
- Payment: Deposit or Full payment toggle cards (B-21: full only when arrival is within balance due days); "Hold until"
  date and time in UAE time.
- Review: summary and "Create booking and email pay link".

Create calls 04-02's `ops_create_booking` (an `awaiting_payment` booking, source `ops`, nights held until the hold
instant, O-03) and then 04-05's `sendPayLink` (O-02). Expired after the hold, lazily.

Output: two pure modules, one handler module, three routes, one screen, pictures, tests. No SQL, no email renderer.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-10 | Elevation | three new routes | mitigate | built only with 03.2's `opsGet` / `opsPost` (`requireOwner()` first, Origin check on POSTs); source check test |
