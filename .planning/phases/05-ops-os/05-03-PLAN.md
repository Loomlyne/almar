## Decisions this plan makes

- **Guest line carries the amount.** "Refunded on {date}" with the amount beside it. `booking_view` gives exactly those two fields for this.
- **`moneyOnClosed` reads net of refunds on the guest's page.** On a cancelled or expired booking the page says "We have received {amount}. We will contact you about it." After a full refund that would be false, so the sentence shows only while paid minus refunded is above 0, with that net amount. The sentence itself is unchanged. Named at the gate.
- **A void sends no email,** as a payment void (P-11). The void dialog tells the owner to tell the guest himself. P5-03 gives one new email only.
- **No owner notice for a refund.** The owner did it himself. P5-03 names a guest email only.
- **Amount is not pre-filled.** Record payment pre-fills what is due; a refund is typed as it left the bank, with what is left to refund in the hint.
- **Refunded and Net received rows show only when `refunded_fils` is above 0,** as Written off does. All refunds voided: the rows go and the struck-through records stay.
- **`link_version` stays in the record route's reply,** because `RefundRecordRow` lists it. The route is owner-only and the screen never reads it.
