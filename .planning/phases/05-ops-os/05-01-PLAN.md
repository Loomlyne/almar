| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-01 | Elevation | every `/api/ops/*` route here | mitigate | built only with 03.2's `opsGet` / `opsPost` (`requireOwner()` before anything); source check over the five files |
| T-05-02 | Tampering (CSRF) | POST status, resend, settings | mitigate | `opsPost` Origin check (403 `wrong_origin`), JSON only, 64 KiB cap; SameSite cookies from job 02 |
