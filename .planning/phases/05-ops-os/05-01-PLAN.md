## Migration specification (Task 1 implements this)

**Copy rule.** Each replaced function is copied line for line from the table above with `sed -n`, then only the change below
is made. Retyping is forbidden. Each keeps its `revoke` and `grant` lines. Every new function: `language plpgsql`,
`security definer`, `set search_path = ''`, revoke execute from public, anon, authenticated, grant execute to service_role.
No new table, so RLS stays on `booking_refunds` and `bookings` with nothing granted, as they are. New constraints are added
`not valid` then validated, as the neighbours do.

**1. `booking_refunds`.** Drop NOT NULL on `stripe_refund_id` and keep its existing unique constraint. Add `method` text not
null default 'card' check in ('card','bank_transfer'), `refunded_on` date, `bank_reference` text check 1..100 characters,
`reason` text check 3..300, `recorded_by` uuid, `request_id` uuid with constraint `booking_refunds_request_id_key` unique,
`void_reason` text check 3..300, `voided_at` timestamptz, `voided_by` uuid. Checks: `booking_refunds_source` (method 'card'
exactly when stripe_refund_id is not null); `booking_refunds_bank_fields` (a bank row has status in ('succeeded','voided'),
amount_fils at least 1, and refunded_on, recorded_by, request_id, bank_reference not null; card rows keep their free Stripe
status text); `booking_refunds_voided_fields` (coalesce(status,'') = 'voided' exactly when voided_at, voided_by and void_reason
are all set).

Why not a partial unique index: `record_refund` (still deployed, granted and pinned by `bookings-and-ops.test.sql`) says
`on conflict (stripe_refund_id)`, and Postgres refuses that against a partial index unless the statement repeats its predicate.
The existing unique constraint already lets many NULLs through, so it gives the partial rule with `record_refund` untouched.
`record_refund` is not replaced: `method` defaults to 'card', so its insert still satisfies every check.

**2. `bookings.refunded_fils`** bigint not null default 0 check (>= 0). Deliberately no check against `paid_fils`: the rule is
enforced in the three functions below and a table check would turn a refusal into a 503.

**3. Event actions.** Drop and re-add `booking_events_action_check` with the 20 actions of 20261019160000 lines 114-118 plus
`refund_recorded` and `refund_voided`: 22.

**4. `ops_record_refund(p jsonb, p_actor uuid) returns jsonb`.** `booking_assert_owner` first. Keys exactly: booking_id,
request_id, amount_fils, refunded_on, bank_reference, reason (any other is `invalid` with that key). amount_fils 1..1e12.
Follow the replay loop of `ops_record_payment` lines 1134-1174 and 1237-1252: look for the request id without the lock, take
`booking_lock`, look again, insert, on unique_violation of `booking_refunds_request_id_key` retry once. A replay with another
booking or amount is `changed`; otherwise it answers with first_time false and no write. Under the lock: paid minus refunded
at or below 0 is `nothing_to_refund`; amount above it is `above_paid`. refunded_on real, not after today in Dubai, not before
the booking's Dubai creation day, else `invalid` refunded_on. bank_reference trimmed 1..100. reason null or trimmed 3..300.
Insert method 'bank_transfer', status 'succeeded'. Sum `refunded_fils` again from succeeded bank rows, never add to the old value.
Status, nights, `paid_fils`, `written_off_fils`, deadline: unchanged (P-06). Clear the flag when `attention_reason` is 'overpaid'
and paid minus refunded is at or below grand, or is 'paid_after_expiry' or 'paid_after_cancel' and paid minus refunded is 0.
`deadline_passed_part_paid` stays. Event `refund_recorded`, actor `owner:<uuid>`, detail: refund_id, amount_fils, refunded_on,
bank_reference, reason, paid_fils, refunded_fils, request_id, attention_cleared. Answer: see `RefundRecordRow` below.

**5. `ops_void_refund(p_refund bigint, p_reason text, p_actor uuid) returns jsonb`.** B-25. Same opening as `ops_void_payment`
lines 1401-1410: owner, reason trimmed 3..300, find the booking (`not_found`), lock, row for update. Already voided answers
first_time false. Anything but a succeeded bank row is `not_voidable`. Set status 'voided', void_reason, voided_at, voided_by.
Sum again. If `needs_attention` is false after the sum, raise it: 'paid_after_expiry' on expired and 'paid_after_cancel' on
cancelled when paid minus refunded is above 0, else 'overpaid' when paid minus refunded is above grand; write a `needs_attention`
event with reason and refund_id. Event `refund_voided`: refund_id, amount_fils, reason, refunded_fils, attention_raised.
Status unchanged. Answer: `RefundVoidRow` below.

**6. `booking_flags`.** One line: `'overpaid', ((b.paid_fils - b.refunded_fils) > b.grand_fils)`. Nothing else. `settled_fils`,
`due_now_fils` and `still_due_fils` still read paid plus written off, so a refund never raises what is due (P-06).

**7. `booking_view`.** Add a `v_refunds` jsonb: the succeeded bank rows as `refunded_on` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `amount_fils` only,
ordered by refunded_on, created_at, id. Add `'refunds', v_refunds` after `'payments'` and `'refunded_fils'` after `'paid_fils'` in
money. Extend the comment above it: never a refund's reason or bank reference.

**8. `ops_get_booking`.** Add `v_refunds`: every bank row (voided included), newest first, keys as `OwnerRefund` below, the two
emails read as `recorded_by_email` is read for payments. Add `'refunds'` after `'payments'` and `'refunded_fils'` after
`'paid_fils'` in money.

**9. `ops_create_booking`, agreed reason (P5-02).** The reason's storage: an optional payload key `agreed_reason`, read here,
removed with `p - 'agreed_reason'` before the one call to `booking_write`, and written into the `created` event's detail with
`agreed_nights_fils`. Why this: it is atomic with the booking (a booking and its reason commit or fail together, and the
idempotent replay returns before any second event), it lands in History where P5-02 wants it, and `booking_write` stays
untouched (its key whitelist would refuse the key if it were not removed). `log_booking_event` afterwards was rejected: a crash
between the two leaves an agreed price with no reason. Rule, after the replay check: when any `nights_snapshot` element has
source 'agreed', all must, and `booking_txt(p, 'agreed_reason', 3, 300, true)` applies; when none does, the key present is
`invalid` agreed_reason. Non-agreed bookings get the same event detail as today. The rest of the function is identical.

**10. `ops_record_payment`, one line beyond your list.** Line 1277 becomes paid minus `v_b.refunded_fils` above grand. Why: a
late payment after a refund must not raise a false overpaid while `booking_flags` says false (P-06).

**11. `ops_void_payment`, one guard and one compare.** Why: voiding a payment could leave refunds above what was paid, against
B-31. After `v_paid_after` is known (line 1434), before any write: `v_paid_after` below `v_b.refunded_fils` is `refund_first`
(void the refund first). At lines 1482-1483 the overpaid and money-flag clears compare paid minus `v_b.refunded_fils`. Nothing else.

**12. `ops_home_numbers(p_from date, p_to date) returns jsonb`,** stable (P5-08). Null or reversed is `invalid` from or to; over
3660 days `invalid` range. All sums `is_test = false`.
- `bookings`: count where (source 'ops' or requested_at is not null) and the Dubai day of coalesce(requested_at, created_at) is in
  range. That excludes held and abandoned holds; a request later cancelled or lapsed still counts as made.
- `received_fils`: sum of succeeded bank `booking_payments.amount_fils` with received_on in range (write-offs never count).
- `refunded_fils`: sum of succeeded bank `booking_refunds.amount_fils` with refunded_on in range.
- `recorded_fils`: received minus refunded (may be negative).
- `outstanding_fils`, `outstanding_count`: sum and count above 0 of `booking_flags(b) ->> 'still_due_fils'`, independent of the range,
  leaving out an unpaid awaiting_payment more than 2 minutes past its deadline (the sweep's rule), so the number holds whatever the cron's timing.
- Also `from`, `to` as YYYY-MM-DD.

**13. `ops_list_customers(p_q text, p_after text, p_limit int) returns jsonb`,** stable (P5-09). Checks as `ops_list_bookings`
lines 1665-1680 (q at most 100, after 1-6 digits, limit 1..60; defaults null, null, 30). Rows: `is_test = false` and (source 'ops' or
requested_at is not null), so an abandoned hold is not a customer. Group by email (stored lower case by a check). `name` and
`phone` from the newest booking (created_at desc, id), `bookings` count, `last_arrive` the latest arrive. Search: `media_fold` needle
found in any of the group's names, its email or any phone. Order: last_arrive desc, email. Answer `{ items, next }`, next the offset
as text or null.

**Bookings by email (P5-09).** No new filter. `p_q` already folds and searches the email, and `bookings-screen.tsx` line 47 already reads `q`
from the URL through `parseListQuery`. Limits 05-04 handles: a match is a substring, so `a@x.com` also shows `aa@x.com`; `q` caps at 100
characters, so an email over 100 gets no link; the list also shows TEST bookings, marked as they are today.
