## Spacing Scale

Declared values (must be multiples of 4):

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4px | Icon gaps, inline padding |
| sm | 8px | Compact element spacing, gap inside a group |
| md | 16px | Default element spacing, gap between groups |
| lg | 24px | Section padding |
| xl | 32px | Layout gaps |
| 2xl | 48px | Major section breaks |
| 3xl | 64px | Page-level spacing |

Exceptions: none.

Hit targets, radii, borders, breakpoints, type sizes, the booking-bar height, the bar width, and the sidebar width are not spacing tokens. They are specified outside this section. Do not add any other value to this scale.

Group with space, not lines. The gap between groups is at least twice the gap inside a group: 8px inside, 16px between. A hairline is only for a control border, a selected state, or a divider space cannot carry. Use logical properties (`padding-inline`, `margin-inline-start`, `inset-inline-end`). Do not use physical left or right for layout. Do not set a fixed width on text.

## Typography

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 |
| Label | 14px | 400 | 1.4 |
| Heading | 32px | 400 | 1.1 |
| Display | 48px | 400 | 1.1 |

Exactly four sizes. Exactly two weights: 400 and 700.

- 400 is the weight of every role above. Questa is Regular only. Do not synthesize a Questa bold. Noto Naskh Arabic is 400 only. Do not synthesize an Arabic title bold.
- 700 is Lato and Noto Sans Arabic only. Use it for the grand-total amount and for the existing hero Search label (`.hero-search-submit` is already 700). Do not load Hairline, Thin, Light, Medium, Semibold, Heavy, or Black. Do not add a third weight.
- Italic is a style of Lato 400, not a third weight.
- Display and heading tracking: `-0.02em`. Body and label tracking: `0`.
- Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. Descriptions that wrap use `text-wrap: pretty`. Long emails and names use `overflow-wrap: break-word`. Names wrap to two lines, then ellipsis. Prices never truncate.
- Store copy in natural case. Do not store uppercase source strings. The live `/` header is not this phase and keeps its own case.
- Inputs are 16px below `48rem`, so iOS does not zoom. From `48rem` up, inputs are the label size, except the booking bar, which stays 16px Lato at every width. Do not use `transform: scale()` to fake a font size.
- Prices, dates, counts, VAT, deposit, and the FX figure use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Dates are `DD/MM/YYYY`. The week starts Monday. Arabic uses Western numerals.
- Arabic sets `lang="ar"` and `dir="rtl"` on the document. Spanish and English are `ltr`. Isolate a mixed-direction email or amount with `<bdi>`.

The booking bar is Body: 16px Lato, weight 400, line-height 1.5, except the Search label, which stays the existing 700. Do not enlarge the bar to match a larger type size.

## Color

| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| Dominant (60%) | `#fffaf0` | Page, hero fallback, empty surfaces, header after the hero has scrolled past |
| Secondary (30%) | `#ffffff` | Cards, inputs, the booking bar, overlays, the open dropdown |
| Accent (10%) | `#d4ba8a` | The reserved-for list only. Never body text. |
| Destructive | `#8f2d2d` | Danger outline only. Not a fill. Label on that control stays charcoal. |

60% ivory / 30% white / 10% gold. One accent. One notation. `#fffaf0` is the same color as `rgb(255, 250, 240)`. Use the token `--color-ivory`. Do not add a second custom property for the scrolled header.

Teal `#1f3b40` is heading and link ink, and the focus ring. It is not a second accent. Charcoal `#262626` is body text. Status green `#1e6b45` and warning `#8a3b12` are icon colors, not accents and not brand pickers. WhatsApp green `#25D366` is a channel exception, not an accent.

Accent reserved for:

- The one filled primary action on a view (listed in [One filled primary](#one-filled-primary))
- Gold rules between page sections
- The active nav underline
- The confirmed status-chip hairline

Not reserved for: selected stay cards, a gold side bar, the header, Login, Close, Subscribe, Block, every link, every icon, the focus ring, body text, secondary buttons, danger buttons, or the transparent bar.

Do not borrow a token outside its role. Gold is never body text. Red is field-error text and the danger outline only. Unavailable reasons, empty lists, holds, and maintenance stay charcoal.

Primary action fill is gold. Label is teal. Teal on gold is 6.37:1. Hover fill is `--color-accent-hover` `#c6ae82`. Press fill is `--color-accent-press` `#b8a27a`. Never ivory or white on gold. Never gold as text on ivory.

Danger control: transparent fill, charcoal label, 1px `--color-danger` outline. Not a red fill. Not a gold fill.

Focus: `:focus-visible`, 2px solid `--color-focus` (teal), offset 2px. In `forced-colors`, the ring is `CanvasText`. Do not set `outline: none` without that replacement. A field that already has a border keeps the existing pattern: no second outline, border color becomes the heading token.

Do not use color alone for errors, selected rows, status, or the scrolled header. Pair the color with text.

Measured pairs already recorded in `01-UI-SPEC.md` and `02-UI-SPEC.md`. Do not invent a new passing pair. Do not darken the transparent header to invent a contrast pass. See [Header](#header).

## Interaction

- Native `button` or `a`. Do not use a `div` as a control.
- `:focus-visible` 2px teal, offset 2px. Hit target 44px, including compact density and icon-only controls.
- Labels sit above fields. Placeholder is an example, not the name. Required mark is a charcoal asterisk after the label.
- `aria-invalid` on errors. Live region for search results and form status.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no transform, no scale, no header color transition. The static cue remains: ivory header after scroll, darker press fill, sidebar present with scrim.
- Sidebar and menu transitions are interruptible. Name the properties. Do not animate layout on the page under the sidebar.
- Radius stays 0 on controls, overlays, the booking bar, the WhatsApp control, steppers, chips, photos, and the profile photo. `--radius-control` and `--radius-overlay` stay 0. Do not apply a concentric radius. Do not add a 40px pill or a circle. Do not add a radio control.
- Elevation is shadow (`--shadow-overlay`), not a heavier border. Structure is a 1px border. Selected stay cards use neither a gold side bar nor a fill.
- Icons: `currentColor`, stroke 1.5, beside 400 text.
