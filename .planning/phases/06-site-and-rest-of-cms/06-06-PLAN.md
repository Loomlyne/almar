The rest of the public site and the CMS behind it. For this plan: the owner runs her packages from the dashboard, and a
guest opens a package from home, reads its legs and what is included, and asks for it; the owner then prices it and
makes the booking by hand.

| build (anon key) -> `api_journey_tier_*` views | public read of published packages only |

|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-06-01 | Spoofing / Tampering | `/api/requests` kind package | mitigate | same-origin check, content type and size caps, `allowFormPost` rate limit, `HONEYPOT_FIELDS`, `parsePackageRequest` with `SITE_REQUEST_CAPS`, then `site_request_payload_ok` in the table check (06-01) |
