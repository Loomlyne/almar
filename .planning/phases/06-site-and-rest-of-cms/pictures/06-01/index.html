What changes. In every editor, an Arabic or Spanish text you type yourself under Edit translations stays yours: a later change to the English does not replace it. Under such a box there is one plain line with a small lock, and Translate again at its end (on the left in Arabic) to hand that one box back to the AI. Nothing else on the page moves. Example: the Cartagena destination, with its real texts; "قرطاجنة" and "Cartagena de Indias" are the hand-typed names. Drawn with the real tokens (teal primary, gold underline on text buttons, square corners, no badge, no radio).
  1. lock-one-en-1440: under the Arabic name, "Your own text. A change to the English will not replace it." and Translate again at the end; the Spanish box has no line.
  2. lock-one-ar-390: the same in Arabic, right to left, Translate again on the left.
  3. lock-refilling-en-834 and lock-refill-failed-en-834: "Translating…" while it works; if the AI fails, "Could not translate now. It will be filled when you save." and the box empty.
  4. lock-none-es-1440: the page as today, no line anywhere.

Arabic and Spanish wording of the new lines is our draft (dashboard Spanish uses usted); change any word in your reply. Screen-reader only: a locked box is announced with "locked" / "مقفل" / "bloqueado" after its label.
