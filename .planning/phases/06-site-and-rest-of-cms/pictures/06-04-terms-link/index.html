Booking terms: the link in the request step

Plan 06-04, for signature. Titles shown are samples; on the site the words are the title you publish. A dashed gold outline marks what is new and is not part of the design.

1. The terms box when your terms are live

The title you publish becomes a link to your Booking terms page. What the guest typed is safe: the link opens the terms in a new tab, so the booking stays open on this tab with every field and the box as they were. Clicking the link does not tick the box. A screen reader also hears "(opens in a new tab)".

2. The terms box with no terms live

As it is today: no link, and real requests stay closed. This also shows in the minutes between you publishing and the site being rebuilt.

3. Spanish: the words before the title (choose one)

Today's Spanish, "Acepto los", only fits a masculine plural title. A (Recommended): "Acepto:" works with any title you choose. B keeps "Acepto los" and breaks if your title is "Condiciones de reserva".

4. The footer on the booking pages

The booking and trip pages get the same Legal row as the rest of the site, under Pages and Contact, as you signed in 06-04. With nothing published the row is not there and the footer is as it is today.
