What this adds. Dashboard → Content → Blog replaces its Coming soon page: the list of posts with a search box, and the editor you already use with a Body made of blocks (heading, paragraph, photo, quote) that you add, move and delete. English only; Arabic and Spanish are filled on Save, and a block you fix by hand stays locked. On the public blog, a search box above the stories, and a photo can sit inside a post. The three imported posts are the real ones; [brackets] mark a sample value.
  1. blog-list-en-1440: Blog heading, New post, Search posts, three rows (title, Published, date), newest first, no Soon chip.
  2. blog-blocks-ar-390: right to left; one heading, paragraph, photo (with its alt line) and quote, each with Move up, Move down and Delete; the four Add buttons under them; square corners.
  3. blog-imported-published-en-834: the address is a fixed line with "The address is fixed once the post has been published."; the Arabic title shows the lock line and Translate again.
  4. blog-publish-missing-es-1440: Publish waits; the list names the cover photo and the Spanish text of block 2 (usted wording).
  5. blog-search-match-en-390 and blog-search-none-ar-834: the box above the cards; a match leaves only matching stories with a count; no match says so with Clear.
  6. blog-photo-block-es-1440: the photo in the text column between paragraph and quote, square, no caption.

Every dashboard word is new copy (lib/copy/ops-blog.ts): English is ours, Arabic and Spanish are drafts for you (dashboard Spanish uses usted). The four public search lines (lib/copy/blog.ts) are new too; the public site keeps its own voice (Spanish tú). Change any word in your reply.
