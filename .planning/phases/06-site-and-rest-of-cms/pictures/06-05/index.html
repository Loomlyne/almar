Public: a Plan with us page (its own page, linked from the home Begin section and from Contact), a List with us page (linked from the footer only), and a 30-minute Consultation calendar on Contact (times shown in the guest's own time zone with the Dubai time beside each). Dashboard: Requests (every Plan, List, Package and Consultation, with filters and Handled / Not handled) and Content → Consultation (weekly Dubai hours, days off, minimum notice, booking horizon).
