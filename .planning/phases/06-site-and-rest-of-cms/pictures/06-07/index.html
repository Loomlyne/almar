What this adds. A heart on every stay card (home Private Stays, the Private Stays list, More Private Stays on a stay page) and on the stay page itself, a Share control on the stay page, and a Saved stays card on Account. Signed out, the heart is a link to sign-in and the stay is saved when she comes back. Stays, photos and names are the real published stays from the site data; nothing here is a price, a person or legal text. Arabic and Spanish are draft for signature; the full list of new lines is at the bottom.
