## The three that matter most

**4 is now the critical path, not a 3.2 gate item.** Criterion 5 says the Supabase swap may change only
`lib/data/<entity>.ts`. So the fixture types job 06 writes must already be the final translation shape. If job 06
picks "three columns per field" and 3.2 later wants "one row per language", criterion 5 cannot be met without
editing components — the exact thing it forbids. The prep recommended one translation row per language per item.
**This has to be settled before job 06 writes `lib/data`, not at the 3.2 discuss gate.** It compounds with the
per-locale URL decision: the data layer has to expose a per-locale read, because the pages are built three times.

**2 is void, and it deletes work.** Its whole premise was "a Publish button in 3.2 would publish into nothing,
because the public pages are Framer HTML until 3.3 and Phase 6". After the reshape the real pages exist *before*
3.2 runs. So the owner-only preview pages (recommended option A) are unnecessary, and success criterion 3 does not
need rewording — "public Team reads only published members" is directly achievable. Do not build the preview layer.

**12 is void because he restated the rule.** 13:30: "every control shown must work." That settles hide-vs-disable
in favour of hide, and it is no longer a question to put to him.
